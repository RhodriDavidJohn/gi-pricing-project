# Results

_Generated by `scripts/report.py` on 18 September 2026. Every number below is calculated from this run; the Discussion section is for the author's interpretation._

## Summary

- **Data:** 678,013 policies, 358,499 policy years, 36,102 claims and 60,697,931 of claims cost (0.101 claims and 169 of cost per policy year).
- **Test set:** 135,603 policies (20%), used only for the results below.
- **Selected GLM:** Poisson frequency × log-normal severity. Interactions: frequency `VehAgeBand:VehBrand`, `NewVehAge:YoungDrivAge`; severity none.
- **Selected XGBoost:** hurdle frequency × severity with `reg:gamma` objective.
- **Ranking:** GLM has the higher test Gini (0.2578 vs 0.2403, a difference of 0.0174).
- **Where the models disagree** (outer double lift bands), the XGBoost premium is closer to actual experience (average gap 39.7 vs 92.5 percentage points of average cost).
- **Loss ratio on test** after rebasing on the validation set: GLM 0.945, XGBoost 0.937.
- **Switching from GLM to XGBoost** would change the premium by more than 10% for 69.4% of test policies (28.6% up, 40.8% down).
- **Large claims:** the largest 1% of claims (over 16,794) make up 38.0% of claims cost.

## Limitations

- Prices are pure premiums (expected claims cost); expenses, commission and profit aren't included.
- Severity models are fitted to claim amounts capped at the 99.5th percentile (34,387); the cost above the cap is spread over all policies by the rebase factor rather than priced by risk. Test loss ratios use uncapped claims, so a few big claims still move them.
- Results come from a single train/validation/test split, without confidence intervals.
- Customer details used by the models: DrivAge, BonusMalus, VehAge, VehPower, VehBrand, VehGas, Density, Area, Region.
